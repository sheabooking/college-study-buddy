## What This Is

College Study Buddy turns Claude into a study assistant that actually remembers where you left off — part file organizer, part accountability partner, part cheerleader. It's not a tutor and it's not a generic chatbot. It's a smart friend who knows the material, knows your course folder, and meets you exactly where you are on a given day.

Say `/study` and it picks up the thread: what you covered last time, what you said you'd do next, how you were feeling. No re-explaining your situation every session.

---

## What It Actually Does

- **Remembers your sessions** — keeps a running study log per student, so every session starts with real context instead of a blank page
- **Organizes your course files** — spots loose files sitting in Downloads or on your Desktop and offers to file them into the right course folder
- **Builds a real study plan** — not just a to-do list, but a day-by-day schedule with time blocks, built around platform constraints (grading waits, active-course limits) and your actual weekly availability
- **Keeps you honest on academic integrity** — helps you think through assignments, brainstorm, and tighten your own writing, without ever generating submittable work for you to turn in as your own
- **Meets you emotionally where you are** — frustrated, behind, burnt out, or on fire — without judgment and without rushing past how you're actually feeling
- **Helps with AI-use citations** — walks you through APA and MLA formats for citing AI assistance, including the gray areas around terminal-based tools
- **Supports multiple students** — parents, tutors, and homeschool coordinators can run separate profiles without ever mixing progress between kids

## Install This Skill

**Option 1 — Download and install:**
1. Download `college-study-buddy.plugin` from this repo
2. Run:
```bash
claude plugin install /path/to/college-study-buddy.plugin
```

Once installed, type `/study` to start a session, or just start talking about your coursework — the skill picks up on studying, homework, feeling behind, or dropping in a course file, even if you never type the slash command.

## Who This Is For

- Students juggling multiple self-paced or online courses
- Anyone who loses track of "where did I leave off" between study sessions

- Parents or tutors managing more than one student's coursework
